AML TRANSACTION MONITORING - ANALYST INVESTIGATION GUIDE
===========================================================================

PURPOSE
This guide provides a structured investigation workflow for AML transaction-monitoring alerts.

STEP 1 - REVIEW ALERT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Review alert score, priority, alert type, rule signals, ML score and SHAP drivers.

STEP 2 - REVIEW CUSTOMER PROFILE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Review customer type, occupation, expected activity, risk rating, PEP indicator, sanctions indicator and adverse-media indicator.

STEP 3 - REVIEW TRANSACTION ACTIVITY
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Review transaction amounts, frequency, counterparties, cash activity, international activity, geographic activity and transaction timing.

STEP 4 - REVIEW RED FLAGS
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Assess whether identified red flags have a reasonable explanation based on the customer's known profile and expected activity.

STEP 5 - REVIEW MODEL EXPLANATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use SHAP drivers to understand which behavioral features contributed to the model's risk prediction.

STEP 6 - CUSTOMER CONTEXT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Consider whether the activity is consistent with the customer's occupation, income, expected volume, geography and known business purpose.

STEP 7 - DISPOSITION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Possible analyst dispositions in this project include:
  - FALSE_POSITIVE
  - CLEARED
  - MONITOR
  - INVESTIGATE_FURTHER
  - ESCALATE

STEP 8 - SAR CONSIDERATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
SAR consideration should be based on appropriate investigation, applicable requirements, institutional procedures and human review. A machine-learning score alone should not automatically determine SAR filing.

IMPORTANT MODEL GOVERNANCE NOTE
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rules, thresholds and ML scores are risk indicators. They are not legal conclusions and should be reviewed within the institution's governance framework.
